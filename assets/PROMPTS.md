# Gemini prompts for the Groucho clock

Upload the Mirror Scene's Groucho sheet (`~/projects/mirror-scene/assets/src/groucho.jpeg`) with each prompt, so he matches the other screensavers. Save the results in `~/Pictures/groucho-clock/` under the names in the headings.

## 01-face.png

```
A character sheet in exactly the same 1930s rubber-hose cartoon style as the picture I uploaded: thick clean black ink outlines, flat muted colours, a little halftone shading, NOT photorealistic, no text, no watermark. A flat, pure magenta (#FF00FF) background with no magenta anywhere on the drawings, every piece separate with clear magenta space round it, nothing touching or overlapping. The same man as in my picture (black hair parted in the middle, thick black greasepaint moustache, bushy black eyebrows, round wire glasses, cigar), drawn LARGE, seen straight on from the front, head and shoulders, in a black tailcoat and white wing collar with a bow tie, all pieces at the SAME scale so they fit back together:
Piece 1, big, top left: his head and shoulders with NO eyebrows, NO glasses, NO cigar, and his eyes as empty white eyeballs with NO pupils, mouth closed in a sly smile.
Piece 2: the same head's mouth area only, open in a wide grin, showing teeth, as a patch to lay over the closed mouth.
Piece 3: his round wire glasses on their own, the lenses empty and clear (just the frames and the bridge).
Piece 4: his left eyebrow on its own, thick and black. Piece 5: his right eyebrow on its own.
Piece 6: one round black pupil.
Pieces 7 to 10: his fat cigar on its own, held straight out to the side, in four lengths: brand new and unlit; lit with a short grey ash; half burnt with a long ash; a short stub with a long drooping ash.
Piece 11: a small wisp of cigar smoke.
```
