# The Groucho clock: plan (2026-10-14)

A clock you can actually read, and a Groucho to look at:

- Groucho, big, face-on, in his greasepaint moustache and round glasses.
- **His glasses are the clock.** Each lens is a little clock face, numbers round the rim:
  the left lens shows the hour, the right lens the minutes, and his PUPILS are the hands.
  His eyes go round the dial as the time passes, so he always seems to be leering
  sideways at something.
- **Every minute** his eyebrows waggle.
- **The cigar** burns down through the hour, its ash growing; on the hour he taps it
  off and lights a fresh one.
- **On the hour** he says a line from Animal Crackers or The Cocoanuts (both public
  domain), in a title card as in the films: "Hello, I must be going", "One morning I shot
  an elephant in my pajamas...", "Why a duck?".
- **Gummo**, as a tribute: now and then he peeks in round the edge of the picture and
  is gone before you are sure you saw him.
- Real time, like edhar-clock (read_time()).

## Two clocks (the user, 2026-10-14)

Make BOTH:
1. **The cartoon clock**, from the Gemini prompts in assets/PROMPTS.md (matches the Mirror Scene and Duck Soup).
2. **The Monty Python clock**, Terry Gilliam style: real photo cut-outs with hinged and rolling parts. Groucho is cut from a face-on frame of Animal Crackers (1930, public domain), with Victorian engravings for the scenery (the Met's CC0 prints) and Gummo from a public-domain vaudeville photo, if one exists. It matches Captain Spaulding.
